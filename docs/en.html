BEYOND THE PICTURE

Capture the picture.
Understand it, too.

Extract text locally. Connect your chosen services when you need translation or AI.

01 / READ

Recognize & translate

Select text to recognize and copy it offline with Apple Vision. Read QR codes and barcodes, too. Text and screen translation share your configured translation service.

02 / ASK

Ask AI about a screenshot

Send a screenshot to a vision model, or extract the text first. Streaming chat, image attachments, and conversation history work with OpenAI Chat / Responses and Anthropic-compatible APIs.

03 / LISTEN

Record & edit audio

Record system audio, your microphone, or both on separate tracks. Pause and resume, then trim, split, and adjust levels in the audio editor before exporting to m4a.

OCR works offline. Translation, AI, and image uploads send the relevant content to your configured services. Review your providers and privacy settings before using them.

LIGHT BY DESIGN / 03

Quiet by design.
Ready when you are.

Lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. Built natively with AppKit and SwiftUI, powered by ScreenCaptureKit, AVFoundation, and Vision. No extra runtimes to install; the WebP encoder is statically bundled.
